Standard symbols, footprints and installed 3D models: KiCad libraries.
https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/
KiCad libraries use CC BY-SA 4.0 with the KiCad library exception. See that
page for terms. Standard DIN pad identifiers were uppercased to match the
host's symbol; the standard footprint pad geometry was preserved.
Used footprints and available STEP models are bundled in project-local libraries.
The nominal RP2350B, DRV8837C and USB-C VRML models in models/ are newly made
review geometry, explicitly not manufacturer models. Earlier review-models/
files are retained for history and are not referenced by the current board.
HRO USB-C: https://www.krhro.com/Product-Details/726.html
JLCPCB stackups: https://jlcpcb.com/impedance
JLCPCB manufacturing capabilities: https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/Capabilities

Current native-board verification used KiCad 10.0.6 and its bundled
Python/pcbnew. The current single-RP2350B layout uses explicit manual routes,
not an autorouter. No design was uploaded to a routing or manufacturing service.
