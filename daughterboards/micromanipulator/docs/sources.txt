Standard symbols, footprints and installed 3D models: KiCad libraries.
https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/
KiCad libraries use CC BY-SA 4.0 with the KiCad library exception. See that
page for terms. Standard DIN pad identifiers were uppercased to match the
host's symbol; the standard footprint pad geometry was preserved.
The approximate body-envelope VRML models in review-models are newly made
review geometry, explicitly not manufacturer models.

Verification used KiCad 10.0.6, its bundled Python/pcbnew, PlatformIO 6.2.0,
and local Freerouting. No design was uploaded to a routing or manufacturing
service. All build and validation artifacts are local.
