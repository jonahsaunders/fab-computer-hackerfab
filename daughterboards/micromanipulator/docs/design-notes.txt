DESIGN NOTES — REV A

Architecture
One bare RP2350B handles motion and tools. The existing host UART and I2C0
contacts are retained. See controller-decision.txt for the resource audit,
software implications, and the PCB/firmware work still required.
The winding-PWM H bridges are retained; changing to STEP/DIR would require
a different control algorithm.

Three TB6612FNG bridges use the backplane 12 V rail. The published 30 ohm
windings draw 0.4 A at 12 V; six continuously energized windings would total
2.4 A before PWM reduction. F2 is a provisional 3 A fuse. Verify winding
temperature, bridge temperature, connector ratings and supply transients.
TB6612FNG's recommended VM maximum is 13.5 V, so the actual 12 V rail, including
regeneration and transients, needs checking at the card. Local bulk capacitors
are present. A fuse alone does not clamp overvoltage.

Power reuse
12 V and 5 V come directly from their assigned DIN contacts through local
branch fuses. There is no daughterboard 24 V converter or 5 V buck regulator.
AP2112M-3.3TRG1 in SO-8 supplies logic 3.3 V from existing 5 V.
The provisional 250 mA allocation gives about 0.50 W at 5.25 V input and
3.25 V output. See power-budget.md for the package choice and thermal estimate.
Verify actual current and temperature with the final loads and enclosure. RP2350 core inductor/capacitors are required MCU support, not a
duplicate backplane supply. 5 V load bulk capacitance and bypass capacitors
remain local because wiring and connector inductance cannot be eliminated.

The backplane's TPS2116 is a 2.5 A power mux. That is a component limit, not a
guaranteed available current for this card. USB power has a separate source
limit. The RP2350B senses the 12 V rail through 100k/22k on GPIO41/ADC1.
The consolidated firmware must inhibit tool loads when that rail is absent. This prevents ordinary USB-only service power
from starting them, but does not detect every supply fault or authorize loads
above the host's capacity. Validate the host mux and upstream supply under
simultaneous loads. The heater, pump and rotation motor ratings remain open.

Tools
DRV8837CDSGR uses the actual TI WSON-8 package with its exposed ground pad.
It replaces the source schematic's TPM8837C-labelled device and mismatched
SO-8 assignment. Its 1 A bridge capability must be checked against the rotation
motor's stall current and the thermal layout. It is not a regulated-current
stepper driver. Recalibrate the source motor's encoder lookup table.

TC4427A buffers the two 3.3 V PWM signals into 5 V gate drive for the AO3400A
MOSFETs. Gate pulldowns keep the switches off during reset.
The vacuum output includes a flyback diode. Select the exact diode and F1
fuse against measured pump startup/stall current and transient energy.
The heater supply passes through J17 for an independent, normally closed
thermal cutoff attached to the heater. Select its trip temperature and
current rating for the actual heater/process. Firmware limits alone are not
an independent thermal cutoff.

Firmware and mechanical implementation status
The firmware is still split across the old RP and STM32 builds. Those images
do not implement the RP2350B tool functions. The complete consolidation and
timing requirements are recorded in controller-decision.txt.

The current PCB matches the single-controller schematic and is manually routed.
It retains the DIN mating geometry and every assigned backplane contact.
The layer order is F.Cu signal, In1.Cu power, In2.Cu ground, B.Cu signal.
All 35 checked local supply paths connect directly on F.Cu. See validation.md
and layout-review.md for the saved-board checks and current review limits.

USB-C J18 has separate CC pull-downs, USBLC6 protection, VBUS sensing and
27 ohm series resistors. VBUS does not power the board. Supply it through
the backplane before USB service. Firmware must implement self-powered USB
and cable detection. USB/ESD behavior still requires bench verification.

Release gates
Confirm the mating DIN part, envelope/retention and sensor magnet geometry;
finish any layout items listed in validation.txt; qualify power and thermal
behavior with the actual loads; verify boot/debug, SPI direction/CRC, UART and
I2C on assembled hardware; then test homing and temperature faults before
closed-loop motion and full-power heater/pump operation. Exact passive,
connector, fuse and thermal-cutoff ordering parts are not yet a sourced BOM.
